# Admin guide

Sign in at **/admin/** with your email, your password and the 6-digit code from your authenticator app. You'll be signed out after 2 hours without activity.

**Owners** can do everything.

**Staff** can:

- manage the waiting list and clients
- edit room descriptions and photos
- edit page content
- draft emails

Only owners can:

- change prices, price basis, capacity or inventory
- change retreat dates, venue and payment terms
- approve emails
- change client emails
- manage staff access

## The tabs

- **Overview.** Waiting list size, opt-ins, activated accounts, confirmed guests, occupancy, payments collected, balances due and the launch checklist.
- **Waiting List.** Search and filter by room, consent, status or source. Click **Edit** to change status or add private notes. **Export CSV** gives everyone. **Export marketing list** gives only people who agreed to updates.
- **Clients.** Everyone on the list or with a portal account. **Send portal invitation** emails them a link to sign in. To change someone's email, first confirm the request with them from their current address. Then an owner uses **Change email**.
- **Rooms and Inventory.** Click a room to edit it.
  - Fill in price basis, capacity, beds, bathroom, privacy and accessibility, and upload real photos with a short description of each.
  - Each **inventory unit** is one real room or bed space you can sell.
  - When everything is checked, the room shows **Ready to sell**.
- **Bookings and Payments.**
  - The launch checklist and the payment terms editor.
  - Reservations can only be opened once every checklist item is done.
  - Saved terms apply to new bookings only. Existing bookings keep the terms they were sold with.
- **Client Experience.** The itinerary (mark sessions provisional until confirmed), announcements for everyone in the portal, the packing list and arrival guidance for confirmed guests, the forms list and the portal text.
- **Content and Branding.** Everything on the public page. This includes:
  - the logo
  - the hero photo and text
  - the grounds gallery
  - the Experience sessions
  - Estar's bio
  - FAQs
  - testimonials
  - contact details
  - waiting-list wording
- **Communications.**
  - Edit email templates and preview them.
  - Owners approve and turn emails on.
  - **Delivery history** shows every email. Failed ones retry automatically, or you can click **Retry now**.
- **Settings and Audit.** Retreat dates, venue, exact address (confirmed guests only), day pass, staff access, connection status and the change history.

## Draft, preview, publish

Changes on **Content and Branding** and **Client Experience** stay in draft until you publish them:

1. **Save draft** keeps your work. Nothing changes on the live page.
2. **Preview** opens the page with your draft. Only signed-in admins can see it.
3. **Publish** makes it live. The previous version is kept in the history.

Text in `[square brackets]` is highlighted on the page as a placeholder until it's replaced. When the content is approved, turn off the **awaiting approval** banner in Content and Branding.

## Things the system won't let you do (on purpose)

- Open paid reservations before the launch checklist is complete.
- Publish the yoni steaming session until an owner records the safety and suitability review.
- Publish a testimonial without recording permission.
- Show contact details until an owner marks them verified.
- Export intentions or staff notes.
- Change the price on a booking that has already been sold.
